## Abstract

<p>The Aurivillius-phase layered material SrBi<sub>2</sub>Ta<sub>2</sub>O<sub>9</sub> possesses an extremely low leakage current (~10<sup>−9</sup> A·cm<sup>−2</sup>) along the out-of-plane direction with in-plane ferroelectricity, which brings difficulties in achieving high ferroelectric performance and low leakage current simultaneously in randomly oriented ceramics. Here, highly textured SrBi<sub>2</sub>Ta<sub>2</sub>O<sub>9</sub> ceramics with a texture degree of <i>f</i> = 0.985 are prepared using templated grain growth (TGG) technology with a tape-casting method. The in-plane remnant polarization achieves 15.83 μC·cm<sup>−2</sup>, which is increased by 197% compared with that of the SrBi<sub>2</sub>Ta<sub>2</sub>O<sub>9</sub> randomly oriented ceramic. Doping Ca is found to reduce the in-plane leakage current density to <i>J</i> = 5.924×10<sup>−7</sup> A·cm<sup>−2</sup>, which is attributed to the increased electronic band gap. This research presents an effective route for developing high-performance SrBi<sub>2</sub>Ta<sub>2</sub>O<sub>9</sub>-based ferroelectric devices.</p>
