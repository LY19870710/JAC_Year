## Abstract

With the rapid deployment of 5G and the emergence of 6G technologies, the demand for high-performance microwave dielectric ceramics (MWDCs) has increased. This study developed Y3MgAl3GeO12 (YMAG) garnet ceramics to meet 5G\/6G requirements for low signal delay, low loss, and high-temperature stability. YMAG ceramics synthesized via a solid-state reaction were characterized for phase composition, crystal structure, microstructure, and microwave dielectric properties. The results revealed that YMAG ceramics exhibited excellent microwave performance: a permittivity (\u03B5r) of 9.86, a quality factor (Q\u00D7f) of 89,000 GHz, and a temperature coefficient of resonant frequency (\u03C4f) of \u221240 ppm\/\u00B0C. Far-infrared and terahertz (THz) spectroscopic analyses verified the low intrinsic dielectric loss and frequency-stable dielectric characteristics of the material in high-frequency ranges. Temperature-dependent dielectric measurements coupled with thermal expansion studies revealed outstanding stability in this material, as evidenced by its low coefficient of thermal expansion (\u03B1L = 9.13 ppm\/\u00B0C). To attain near-zero \u03C4f, we added TiO2 as a positive \u03C4f compensation agent. This strategy effectively tuned the \u03C4f value to within |\u03C4f| \u0026lt; 10 ppm\/\u00B0C while preserving excellent microwave dielectric performance (Q\u00D7f \u2248 43,000 GHz). Furthermore, a rectangular dielectric resonator antenna (DRA) designed with the optimized YMAG\u2013TiO2 composite demonstrated excellent impedance matching (VSWR = 1.02) and high radiation efficiency (\u0026gt; 90%) in the X-band (10.21 GHz), validating its potential for 5G\/6G applications. This work provides a novel approach for developing high-performance MWDCs for next-generation communication technologies and emphasizes the critical role of material design and optimization in achieving superior microwave properties.
